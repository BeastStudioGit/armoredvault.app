PRIVACY POLICY

Your data stays yours.

Zero collection. Zero network access. Zero analytics. Everything lives on your iPad or Mac and nowhere else.

Effective date: June 22, 2026

Overview

Armored Vault is designed with privacy as its core principle. By default the app operates entirely offline and does not collect or share any user data. The sole exception is optional iCloud sync, which you enable yourself — and even then, only ciphertext moves through your own private iCloud, where Apple cannot read it. Beast Studio never collects or receives your data in any case.

Data Collection

Armored Vault collects no data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry are gathered
  • No crash reports are sent automatically
  • No advertising identifiers are used
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are employed
  • No account creation is required

Network Activity

With iCloud sync off, Armored Vault makes zero network connections and communicates with no server, API, or external service — all encryption, decryption, and file storage happens entirely on your device. The one network feature is optional iCloud sync, off until you enable it. When it is on, your device exchanges only ciphertext — encrypted file blobs and an encrypted index — with your own private iCloud. Apple cannot read your files, names, or keys, and there is still no server run by Beast Studio in between.

iCloud Sync

Armored Vault offers an optional iCloud sync feature that keeps one vault current across your own Mac and iPad. It is off by default and runs only after you explicitly turn it on. Sync uses your own private iCloud account (Apple's iCloud / CloudKit) — there is no account system and no server operated by Beast Studio. Only ciphertext ever leaves your device: encrypted file blobs and an encrypted index. Your file names, contents, and encryption keys are never exposed; even the key material that passes through iCloud is itself encrypted and useless without your passphrase. Your decoy vault is deliberately excluded from sync and never touches iCloud. Data synced this way is also subject to Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy.

On-Device Data

All files you import or create within the app are encrypted using AES-256-GCM and stored locally on your device. Your passphrase is used to derive an encryption key on your device; the passphrase itself is never stored, transmitted, or recorded in any form. No data leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it — or turn on optional iCloud sync, which moves only ciphertext to your own private iCloud.

Camera Access

The app requests camera access for two optional features: capturing photos and videos directly into the vault, and the break-in detection feature. All captured images remain on your device, encrypted within the app's secure storage. No images are ever uploaded or transmitted.

Biometric & Device Authentication

If you enable biometric unlock (Face ID or Touch ID), authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on your device. The app also uses Apple's device-owner authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, device passcode, or Mac account password) to gate Vault Reset, the sanctioned clean-slate reset path for forgotten passphrases. No biometric data is accessed or stored by Armored Vault in either case — all evaluation happens within iOS or macOS.

Third-Party Services

Armored Vault uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or ad networks. The only external service it can use is your own private iCloud, and only if you enable optional sync — there is no other cloud service and no server operated by Beast Studio.

What This Policy Covers — and What It Doesn't

This policy describes Armored Vault's own behavior: what the app does and does not do with your data. It does not describe, and cannot speak to, the behavior of:

  • Your operating system (macOS or iPadOS), including features that Apple may add in future updates that observe screen content, the clipboard, accessibility APIs, or rendered text.
  • Third-party software you have installed on your device and granted system permissions to, including but not limited to screen recorders, clipboard managers, accessibility tools, AI assistants, and any other software with elevated privileges.
  • Hardware capture devices physically connected to your device.
  • Actions you choose to take that hand content to other apps or services — clicking a link, sharing to Mail or Messages, copying text and pasting it elsewhere, or printing a document. Those are governed by the apps you hand the content to, not by Armored Vault.

The security of the operating system and software environment in which Armored Vault runs is your responsibility, not Beast Studio's. Armored Vault's promise is about what Armored Vault itself does — and what it does is nothing, beyond what you ask it to.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

GDPR, CCPA, and Similar Regulations

Because Armored Vault collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to provide in response to access, portability, deletion, or opt-out requests under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or similar laws. You retain all rights granted by those laws; we simply have no personal data on which to act.
