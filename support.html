Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions I hear most.

How do I create a strong passphrase?

Use the built-in Generate Passphrase tool in Settings. It rolls true random dice against the EFF Large Wordlist to build a Diceware passphrase of 7, 8, 10, or 12 words. A 10-word Diceware passphrase is ~129 bits of entropy — roughly 25.6 quintillion years to crack at one trillion guesses per second. You can also enter your own passphrase; a live strength meter rates it on the spot and the Save gate requires at least Strong (~80 bits).

What happens if I forget my passphrase?

AES-256 encryption cannot be reversed without your passphrase — your files cannot be decrypted by me, by Apple, or by anyone else. However, the app includes a Vault Reset Zone: tap "Forgot passphrase?" on the lock screen, authenticate with Face ID / Touch ID / device passcode, type RESET, and the current vault mode is wiped so you can start fresh with a new passphrase. This is a clean-slate reset, not a decryption back door — your encrypted files in that vault mode are permanently erased. Launch codes, app settings, the Intruder Log, and the other vault mode are preserved.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

No. By default Armored Vault runs entirely offline — no analytics, no ad networks, no third-party SDKs, and no server I run. The one exception is optional iCloud sync, off until you turn it on yourself: even then, only ciphertext (encrypted file blobs and an encrypted index) moves through your own private iCloud, where Apple can't read it. Nothing else leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

What encryption does the app use?

AES-256-GCM — the same authenticated-encryption standard used by governments and financial institutions. Files are encrypted the moment they enter the vault, using a master key wrapped by a passphrase-derived key (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512, 600,000 iterations, per-vault salt). Decryption happens in a streaming chunked pipeline (4 MB segments) so large videos play back instantly without a pre-decrypt wait.

Can I back up my vault?

Yes — four export paths. Full Armored Vault Backup writes an encrypted snapshot of the entire vault to an external drive or any Files / Finder destination — pair with Restore from Backup on a new device to migrate, encrypted end-to-end. Full Decrypted Export writes every file decrypted to a drive in original formats. Export to Password-Protected Zip creates an AES-256 ZIP that opens in 7-Zip / Keka / WinZip; an optional Hide file names option seals everything inside a single ArmoredVault.zip so the outer archive's index reveals nothing. Export to ZIP is a plain unencrypted archive for trusted destinations.

What external drives are supported?

Any USB-C external SSD. For best results, format the drive as APFS on a Mac before use — APFS supports the full Unicode filename set, recovers cleanly from interrupted writes, and outperforms FAT-family filesystems on large videos.

What devices are supported?

The iPad app runs on any iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. The Mac app runs on any Mac with macOS 14 or later. Armored Vault is a Universal Purchase — buy it once and it unlocks on both your Mac and iPad, with Family Sharing for up to six members of your Apple Family. There is no iPhone version — Armored Vault is desktop-and-tablet territory by design.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@beaststudio.dev with "Armored Vault Support" in the subject line. Please include your device (Mac model + macOS version, or iPad model + iPadOS version), the Armored Vault version (Settings → About), and a description of what you saw versus what you expected.
